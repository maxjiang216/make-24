    how it works
    • Solvable? covers all 1820 sets (1362 solvable, 458 not). Decide whether the set can make 24.
    • Solve covers the 1362 solvable sets. Find a solution in your head.
    • Press any key (or tap the cards) to stop the timer and see the answer, then grade yourself with → (right) or ← (wrong).
    • Spaced repetition: a wrong answer brings the set back within a minute. A right answer under the "fast" time moves it to a longer interval (5 min, 30 min, 4 h, 1 day, 3 days, 8 days, 3 weeks, 2 months). A slow right answer keeps its interval. A set counts as mastered from the 1-day interval on.
    • New sets come in the chosen order, a few at a time, once nothing is due. Progress is saved in this browser.
